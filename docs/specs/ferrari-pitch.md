# Ferrari Pitch — Technique and Spirit

> The structure, through-line and supporting quotes for an artist's pitch to Ferrari: how the work is planned and organised to stay true to the brand's roots, values and philosophy, and how it carries the brand's spirit.

**Status:** Draft outline, 2026-10-09. Quotes still to be checked against primary sources before anything is printed (see *Quotes*).

**The working page:** `/proposal/ferrari` in Colour Brain (cream, one touch of Ferrari red), added 2026-10-10. Its words live in `lib/proposals/ferrari.ts`. It proposes the **AI Sculptures · Flow** collection as the project in section 5: AI images → 3D videos → 3D assets → 3D print (extrusion) → delivered to clients → a 3D gallery to walk through, as technique and spirit in one process.

## Context

The world is changing fast. In this new future, innovation is not a department but a **mindset, a way of life**: a way of staying brave while staying connected to one's roots. The pitch argues that art can reflect a brand's philosophy, and that the artist's way of working, slow and deliberate thinking before bold making, mirrors Ferrari's own union of technique and spirit.

## The through-line

**Theme:** technique vs spirit, and the union of both.

**Thesis sentence**, said in the opening and repeated word for word in the close:

> *Technique gives a dream its form. Spirit gives the form its reason. Ferrari is where the two meet.*

**Three recurring words:** **Roots · Technique · Spirit**. They appear on the title slide, as section markers, and together on the final slide.

**One image through every section:** the engine as the heart (technique) that makes a car alive (spirit).

**The test for every slide:** does it serve the roots? If not, cut it.

**Close mirrors the open:** the change the pitch opens on becomes the ambition it closes on ("the best one is the next one").

## Structure

| # | Section | Question it answers | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Context** | Why now? | The world is changing fast. Innovation is a mindset and a way of life: staying brave and connected to our roots. |
| 2 | **Roots** | What does not change? | Ferrari's philosophy: the engine as the heart, red as identity, never satisfied with the last result. Anything new grows out of the roots, not away from them. |
| 3 | **Technique vs spirit** | What does it take? | Technique: precision, engineering, planning, discipline. Spirit: courage, boldness, passion, drive. Technique alone is cold; spirit alone is a dream with no road. A Ferrari is proof that together they make something alive. Emotional centre: the Dino story (below). |
| 4 | **The artist's method** | How do you work? | As an artist: taking time to think about a project, to plan and organise, and to make sure every choice is aligned with the brand's roots, values and philosophy. Technique in service of spirit. |
| 5 | **The spirit made visible** | What will people feel? | Art reflects the brand's philosophy. The spirit is innovation, boldness, courage, and standing up for our highest potential and our dreams: ambition, focus, passion and drive, made visible. The project itself is shown here. |
| 6 | **Discussion** | Where do we go together? | Open questions, so the pitch ends as a conversation rather than a sale. |

## Felt, not only seen (a small part)

Added 2026-10-10. **"Ferrari is not only seen. It is felt."** A small part of section 5, not a new movement: an immersive room for the museums (Maranello, Modena) that visitors dive into instead of looking at a car behind a rope.

- **Sound:** archive engine notes (a V12 at idle, the Dino V6, a lap at Fiorano), cleaned up with AI and placed in space, so each engine breathes as you pass.
- **Scent:** perfumes composed with a perfumer, one per room: hot oil and fuel, cockpit leather, rubber and warm asphalt, Maranello after rain.
- **Touch:** the engine's low frequencies through the floor; a cast aluminium part you may lay a hand on.
- **Sight:** generative visuals that move with the sound, so the room is never quite the same twice.
- **AI:** the room adapts to each visitor; choose an era or a car and a guide tells the story of a part.

It meets the Out of the Box proposal (outofthebox-art.vercel.app): real components, each standing in a room with its own sound and scent, a living museum. Open: whether it joins the pitch or stays a next step.

## Discussion questions

1. Where does Ferrari's technique end and its spirit begin, or is that border the point?
2. How does a brand stay brave as it grows, without losing its roots?
3. What does "standing up for our highest potential" mean for a team, not only for a driver?
4. How should art reflect a brand's philosophy without illustrating it literally?

## Quotes

Widely attributed to **Enzo Ferrari** and repeated by Ferrari dealer sites and motoring publications. **Check exact wording against his memoir *Le mie gioie terribili* (1962; English: *My Terrible Joys*) before printing.**

| Quote | Section |
|---|---|
| "The best Ferrari ever made is the next one." | Context and close: innovation as a mindset |
| "Ask a child to draw a car, and certainly he will draw it red." | Roots: identity strong enough to become instinct |
| "Race cars are neither beautiful nor ugly. They become beautiful when they win." | Technique vs spirit: beauty earned through performance and courage |
| "I don't sell cars; I sell engines." | Technique: the engine as the heart |
| "The Ferrari is a dream." | Spirit: standing up for dreams |

**Avoid:** "If you can dream it, you can do it." It is often credited to Enzo Ferrari but widely believed to be misattributed.

**Dino Ferrari:** no reliably sourced quotes were found. He works better as a story than as a quote, and the story is the theme itself: Dino died in 1956, aged 24. In late 1955 he had suggested a V6 engine, and he was writing about direct injection and smaller engines while carburettors were the norm. Ferrari later named the engines and a line of cars "Dino" after him, and Enzo dedicated his memoir to him. A young man's idea (spirit), made real through engineering (technique), carried forward as part of the roots. Check the dates against Ferrari's own history pages before use.
