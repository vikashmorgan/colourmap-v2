The chapter loops: the reflection dissolves into the block of cells.

**Between pieces, a ball of stars** (2026-11-09: "I don't like that ball of
light for transitions, it is too bright and loses the effect of many stars",
then "a ball of stars better"). Each form loosens into a wide, slowly turning
ball of faint stars round where the next piece stands, and the stars settle
into the new form in its growing order: from the middle of the block, from
the base of the roots and trees, from the centre of the veins. No piece
starts from one crowded point.

**Pieces on their own, Journey on a key** (2026-11-10: "make the 2 forests
go directly into the forest mode, without the circle at the start, and all

one piece alone: it starts already whole, with no ball of stars, and loops.
Near the end of a loop each grain glides back to where the piece began. The

loop, so the wrap does not show. The fruit starts once it has swelled. The
step bar picks the piece; **Journey** plays the whole chapter as above.

On its own a piece is picked by its name, as presets are on the Art panel,
with no time bar (2026-11-10: "just the presets name pills like in stars,
geometry, except when in journey mode"). The pieces with trees have a small
**↑ / ↓** button on the right: their moving dots all climb, or all come down
(2026-11-10: "dots all moving from bottom to up or from up to bottom with a
small button on the right"); in the Journey half do each. All the page's
letters are gold (2026-11-10: "make all letters golden in all that part").

## Words

Each piece has a short line saying what you see and two lines of
reflection, plain and true:
- plant cells joined through the walls (plasmodesmata)
- root tips sensing water and gravity
- the tree and its branching
- most land plants living with fungi in their roots
- leaves turning light into sugar
- florets set at the golden angle, so the spirals come in Fibonacci numbers
- seeds carried away by animals and wind

The Words key hides them, as on the Journey.

## Acceptance

- `/geometry-field/vegetal` opens on piece 1 alone, looping; the step bar
  picks any piece, which starts whole with no ball of stars; Space pauses.
- **Journey** plays the fifteen pieces as one trip and loops; turned off, it
  stays on the piece it was in.
- The pills read Stars · Geometry · Tunnels · Oils · Big Bang · Journey ·
  Vegetal · Experiment, on the Art panel and the trip pages.
- Every root of piece 2 starts at one base.
- Piece 4 is three-dimensional: its tips spread round the trunk in every
  direction, not in one plane.
- Each piece forms by about halfway through, and every grain pours into the
  next piece with no cut, as in the Journey.
- In the Journey each piece opens as a wide ball of faint stars, never a
  bright point.
- The veins are round and symmetric: eight turns, each mirrored, inside one
  circle.
- No new dependencies, nothing stored beyond the Words choice.
