One self-contained file, `content/statistics/index.html`, served by a route handler like `/sql`. It takes the SQL page's look and behaviour ([spec](sql-class.md)) and keeps its order: School's beige paper, day and night shared through `school-theme`.

## Your slides

When a deck is missing, a dashed box at the bottom of the page says which lectures, with *Upload slides* (several PDFs at once). `Stats2.pdf`, or any PDF whose name has *lecture*, *lesson* or *session* and its number (`lecture02_conditioning_bayes.pdf`), is filed as `Stats{n}.pdf` in the private `coding-slides` folder. The box hides once all three are there.

## Storage

No new table. Marks and notes use the Python page's tables through its API, under keys that start with `stats|`:

- a card: `stats|s{lecture}|{card}`, for example `stats|s2|bayes`
- a lecture in general: `stats|s{lecture}`
- the course in general: `stats|notebook`

The Python page leaves `stats|` notes out of its own lists and Review, as it does `sql|`. The browser copy is written first (`stats-marks`, `stats-notes`), and marks made before signing in are sent up once and never overwrite the account.

## Access

`/statistics` redirects a logged-out visitor to `/login?next=/statistics`. Its card is the first on the School menu, above last trimester's classes, and it is listed above Python in the Grid's Learn hub.

## Notes on Lecture 3 — 2026-11-08

From the questions and comments left on the page.

- **Questions fold under each box.** A box's questions and comments sit in a fold, closed by default, that opens beneath it. It opens by itself when an answer is new.
- **Editing a note.** The pen reads "✎ Edit". If the account refuses an edit, the page now says so; before, the edit looked saved and came back changed on the next load.
- **Highlights.** Six colours: yellow, lime (yellow and green mixed, as when the two overlap), green, orange, pink, blue.
- **Session clock.** A half circle above the right-hand dots:
  - It goes from green to yellow up to 45 minutes, then orange, then red after 60, and it is full at 70.
  - Time counts while the page is in front and something was touched, scrolled or typed in the last 3 minutes.
  - A break of 30 minutes or more starts a new session.
  - A click shows this session, today's total, earlier sessions, and *Start a new session*.
  - The box that opens shows the half wheel again, large, with the session's time in its curve (asked for 2026-11-09: "when I click on the timer I want the half wheel to appear in the box that opens").
  - It is kept on this device (`stats-timer`).
- **The case pad.** *The case* on a box opens a pad on the left with the example the box is about:
  - For a box, that is the last picture shown up to it in the lecture. For an exercise, it is its setup, numbers and picture.
  - When there is room, the reading moves right so the pad sits beside it.
  - It follows as you scroll. *From …* goes to the box it came from.
