## Why

> 2026-11-09: "after vegetal series prepare the Ocean series. with corals
> growing like trees. swarms of fishes. swarms of small fishes. jellyfish
> swarms. and entire ecosystem in 3d as the final one with corals fishes
> etc... that we drive through like for the tree road."

The second of the three worlds ([three-worlds.md](three-worlds.md)) to get
its own chapter, built as Vegetal is ([art-vegetal.md](art-vegetal.md)): one
trip in numbered pieces, the step bar jumping to any of them.

## The five pieces

| # | Piece | What you see | Camera |
|---|-------|--------------|--------|
| 1 | Coral garden | Seven coral colonies grow up from the sand like trees, branching at the golden angle; every third is a flat sea fan. Polyps open and close at the tips, light falls in shafts, marine snow drifts up | Slowly round the garden |
| 2 | Shoal | Thirty-six fish, blue backs and silver bellies with a few golden ones, loop through the water as one; each follows the path a moment behind the fish ahead, so turns run through the shoal | Beside the shoal |
| 3 | Small fish | Nine hundred small fish wheel in a ring, gather into a tight ball, and open into the ring again. Their sides flash silver as they turn toward the light | Face on |
| 4 | Jellyfish | Thirteen jellyfish rise slowly, bells pulsing (a quick pull, a slow release), ten trailing tentacles and four frilled arms each, glowing violet and cyan | From below, looking up a little |
| 5 | Reef | A channel through the reef, swum a little above the sand: corals on both sides are small far ahead and grow as we come, a school of small fish wheels ahead, jellyfish drift above, light shafts fall in. The channel never ends: passed corals go round to the far end | Down the middle of the channel |

Between pieces, a ball of faint stars, as in Vegetal. The chapter loops: the
reef dissolves into the coral garden.

## Words

Each piece has a line saying what you see and two lines of reflection:
- corals as colonies of tiny animals building forests of stone
- a shoal with no leader, each fish following its neighbours
- small fish safer packed close, their flash a shared defence
- jellyfish, more than five hundred million years old, with no brain
- a reef: under a hundredth of the sea floor, a quarter of sea life
