## The eight pieces

| # | Piece | What you see | Camera |
|---|-------|--------------|--------|
| 1 | Coral garden | Seven coral colonies grow up from the sand like trees, branching at the golden angle; every third is a flat sea fan. Polyps open and close at the tips, light falls in shafts, marine snow drifts up | Slowly round the garden |
| 2 | Sea fans | Nine sea fans, taller and finer than the garden's, each a single lace of branches, grow up from the sand and all bend together in the current, more toward the top, the far ones a moment later. Violet, magenta, indigo and deep red, no yellow; they are grown in about 12 s, so the swaying starts soon (2026-11-10: "dont put the yellow greens in sea fans. i want them to start moving faster") | Low among the fans, looking up |
| 3 | Table corals | Six table corals: a short stalk grows, then a wide plate of fine branches spreads from its top, its rim curling up, polyps along the rim, at different heights like tiers. Five round brain corals on the sand between them, folded into winding ridges | From above |
| 4 | Fish pulse | Three corals grown close into one thicket stand still, already grown. Twenty-eight clownfish, orange with three white bands, shelter in it; on one beat (every 8 s) they all come out together, each a hair behind the next, hang in the open water, turn and slip back in, then rest and turn out again | Close to the thicket, drifting round very slowly |
| 5 | Shoal | Five kinds of shoal, picked by pills, each of one kind of fish only: **Silver fish** (thirty-six, blue backs and silver bellies with a few golden ones), **Yellow tangs** (forty-four flat yellow discs), **Barracudas** (twenty-two long thin silver fish with dark bars), **Hammerheads** (eight sharks with the hammer head, a tall dorsal fin, side fins and a crescent tail) and **Swordfish** (five, dark blue backs, the long bill, a crescent tail). The bigger the fish, the fewer, the slower and the wider apart, and the eye stands further back. Each fish follows the path a moment behind the fish ahead, so turns run through the shoal | Beside the shoal |
| 6 | Small fish | Nine hundred small fish wheel in a ring, gather into a tight ball, and open into the ring again. Their sides flash silver as they turn toward the light | Face on |
| 7 | Jellyfish | Thirteen jellyfish rise slowly, bells pulsing (a quick pull, a slow release), ten trailing tentacles and four frilled arms each, glowing violet and cyan | From below, looking up a little |
| 8 | Reef | A channel through the reef, swum a little above the sand: corals on both sides are small far ahead and grow as we come, a school of small fish wheels ahead, jellyfish drift above, light shafts fall in. The channel never ends: passed corals go round to the far end | Down the middle of the channel |

The page opens on one piece alone, picked by name, as Vegetal's do
(2026-11-10: "then do the ocean after vegetal"): it starts at its beginning
with no ball of stars and loops, each grain gliding back to where the piece
began; the reef swims on through its channel, so its loop does not show.
**Journey** plays the eight as one trip: between pieces a ball of faint stars,
and the reef dissolves into the coral garden.

Added 2026-11-10: "do more coral gardens. and gardens where the coral is
stable and grown and small fish pulse in and out of it. like clownfish that
all come out together in the same pulse, then go back in. call it fish

shoal types with each time a group of only same fish. some fish more
developed like hammer sharks", "swordfish instead of dolphin" (the Shoal's
five kinds). The kind of shoal is the Shoal's own choice: the Journey always
swims the silver fish.

## Words

Each piece has a line saying what you see and two lines of reflection:
- corals as colonies of tiny animals building forests of stone
- sea fans growing across the current, never along it
- table corals growing outward more than upward, a roof of stone
- clownfish rarely straying far from home, out together and back together
- a shoal with no leader, each fish following its neighbours
- small fish safer packed close, their flash a shared defence
- jellyfish, more than five hundred million years old, with no brain
- a reef: under a hundredth of the sea floor, a quarter of sea life

## Acceptance

- `/geometry-field/ocean` opens on piece 1 alone, looping; the name pills pick
  any piece, which starts with no ball of stars; Space pauses.
- **Journey** plays the eight pieces as one trip and loops.
- The pills read Stars · Geometry · Tunnels · Oils · Big Bang · Journey ·
  Vegetal · Ocean · Experiment, on the Art panel and the trip pages.
- The corals grow from the sand during pieces 1 to 3; in Fish pulse they stand grown.
- In Fish pulse every clownfish is out at the middle of a beat and home at its end, all together.
- The Shoal offers five pills, one kind of fish each; hammerheads have the hammer, swordfish the bill.
- The shoal stays together and points one way.
- Every jellyfish pulses between open and drawn in.
- The eye swims down the reef channel with corals on both sides.
- In the Journey each piece opens as a wide ball of faint stars and every grain pours into
  the next piece with no cut.
- No new dependencies.
