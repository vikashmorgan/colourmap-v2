The manifesto

What we believe

  1. Imagination steers the world. We choose to steer it toward paradise.
  2. Nobody here is too much. Big ideas are welcome at our table.
  3. Madness becomes wisdom when it is shared and made useful.
  4. We make things with our hands and with new tools. Marble and machine, together.
  5. Freedom is time, movement and finding your people. We build all three.
  6. When we gather, dance and create in the open, love wins.

The network

A label you can trust

Love Wins links people who do amazing things: event makers, artists, musicians, spaces. At the start, every person and every event passes through us, so the name always means the same thing.

  1. 1Propose

    Show us what you make

    An event, a space, a craft. Tell us what you want to bring and who it is for.

  2. 2Meet

    We get to know you

    A coffee, a studio visit, a night out. We look for people aligned with the spirit, not follower counts.

  3. 3Carry the heart

    You're in the network

    Your event goes out under the Love Wins label and connects to the right people across the constellation.
